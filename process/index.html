Our Process

Six stages from idea to impact.

A transparent, engineering-led process. Each stage ends with something you can see, question and approve, so there are no surprises at the end.

Process stages

  1. 1Discover
  2. 2Strategize
  3. 3Design
  4. 4Engineer
  5. 5Validate
  6. 6Launch & Evolve

  1. Stage 01

    Discover

    Understand the business, the problem, the audience and the goals.

    We learn how your business works today, who the users are, what is getting in the way and what success should look like. Constraints such as budget, timeline and existing systems are put on the table early.

    What happens

    • Stakeholder conversations
    • Problem and goal definition
    • Review of existing systems and data
    • Requirements gathering

    You receive

    • Shared problem statement
    • Success criteria
    • Initial requirements

  2. Stage 02

    Strategize

    Define the solution, architecture, priorities and delivery roadmap.

    We turn what we learned into a plan: the shape of the solution, the technical direction, what to build first and how delivery will be staged. Trade-offs are explained so decisions are made together.

    What happens

    • Solution and architecture options
    • Technology selection
    • Scope and prioritisation
    • Delivery roadmap

    You receive

    • Architecture outline
    • Prioritised scope
    • Roadmap and estimate

  3. Stage 03

    Design

    Create user journeys, wireframes, interfaces and visual systems.

    We design how the product works before writing production code: user journeys, wireframes and interfaces, plus the data models and integrations behind them. Designs are reviewed with you and refined early, when changes are cheap.

    What happens

    • User journeys and flows
    • Wireframes and interface design
    • Design system
    • Data and API design

    You receive

    • Approved designs
    • Component system
    • Technical design notes

  4. Stage 04

    Engineer

    Build the frontend, backend, integrations and AI capabilities.

    We build in short, reviewable increments: front end, back end, integrations and AI components. You see working software regularly, not just a status update at the end.

    What happens

    • Frontend and backend development
    • Integrations and APIs
    • AI and data components
    • Regular demos

    You receive

    • Working increments
    • Source code in version control
    • Technical documentation

  5. Stage 05

    Validate

    Test functionality, performance, accessibility, reliability and quality.

    Before release we test that everything works as specified, performs well, is accessible and handles errors gracefully. AI components are evaluated on data they have never seen.

    What happens

    • Functional and regression testing
    • Performance and accessibility checks
    • Security review
    • Model evaluation where relevant

    You receive

    • Test results
    • Resolved issues
    • Release readiness

  6. Stage 06

    Launch & Evolve

    Deploy, monitor and keep improving the product over time.

    We prepare and run the deployment, monitor the product once it is live and plan the next improvements based on real usage. Handover documentation means your team is never locked out.

    What happens

    • Deployment and launch
    • Monitoring and support
    • Feedback and analytics review
    • Iteration planning

    You receive

    • Live product
    • Handover documentation
    • Improvement roadmap

Process questions

How long does each stage take?

It depends on scope. A small project may move through Discover and Strategize in days; a complex product takes longer. The roadmap from Strategize gives you concrete estimates for your project.

How involved do we need to be?

Most involved in Discover and Design, where your knowledge of the business matters most. During Engineer, short regular reviews of working software keep the project on track.

What happens after launch?

Launch & Evolve covers monitoring, fixes and improvements based on real usage. Ongoing support is agreed separately so it fits your needs.
