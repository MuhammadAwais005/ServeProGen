## Overview

A web platform for gyms and their members that brings day-to-day gym management together with AI-assisted guidance. The system is **under active development**; the capabilities below describe the scope being built, not a finished, deployed product.

## The problem

Gym members often train without feedback on their form or a plan that adapts to them, while gym staff juggle member records, schedules and recommendations across separate tools.

## The approach

A React front end and a Django back end provide the management features, with Python-based AI components for recommendations and exercise feedback.

- **Management:** member and gym records in one system.
- **Recommendations:** fitness suggestions, plus BMI-based exercise and diet guidance.
- **Pose-based feedback:** exercise form analysis with repetition and accuracy tracking.
- **Planning:** workout history and weekly workout plans.
- **Discovery:** filtering to find nearby gyms.

## Technology

React · Django · Python · SQLite
