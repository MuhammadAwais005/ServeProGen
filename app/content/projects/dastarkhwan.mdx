## Overview

Dastarkhwan is a restaurant software-as-a-service product. Guests scan a QR code to open the menu, and each restaurant on the platform runs its own experience.

## The problem

Restaurants need a simple way for guests to see an up-to-date menu at the table, and a platform serving several restaurants must keep each one's menu and kitchen activity separate.

## The approach

- **QR-based menus:** guests open the menu from a QR code, without installing an app.
- **Restaurant-specific experiences:** each restaurant presents its own menu and identity.
- **Table-specific access:** a menu link is tied to the table it was opened from.
- **Kitchen isolation:** in a multi-restaurant setup, each kitchen sees only its own restaurant's activity.
