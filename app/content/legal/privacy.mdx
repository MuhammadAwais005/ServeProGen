## What we collect

### Information you send us

When you use the contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, business email address, company name (optional), the service you are interested in, your budget range (optional) and your project description.

### Technical information

Like any website, our hosting provider's servers record standard request information, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in server logs for security and reliability. To limit abuse of the contact form, we temporarily store a one-way hash of the sender's IP address to count submissions per hour. The hash is not linked to your inquiry and is deleted automatically.
