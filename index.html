03 / AI & Computer Vision

From pixels to decisions.

We build the whole path: data preparation, model training and honest evaluation, through to software that turns predictions into action, on servers or on edge devices.

  1. 01 FrameImages or video arrive from a camera, upload or stream.
  2. 02 PrepareFrames are resized, normalised and batched for the model.
  3. 03 InferA trained detector or classifier runs on cloud or edge hardware.
  4. 04 DecideDetections become events, counts or alerts in your software.
