Before you write

What should I include in my message?

The problem you're trying to solve, who it's for, any existing systems involved and, if you have them, rough timing and budget. A few sentences is enough to start.

Do I need a detailed specification first?

No. Turning an idea into a clear specification is part of Discover and Strategize. Bring the problem; we'll help shape the solution.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. Your details are used only to respond to your inquiry. See the privacy policy for how contact form data is handled.
