Common questions

How does a project start?

With a conversation about the problem, not the technology. Send an inquiry through the contact form and we'll reply with questions and a suggested next step.

Do you work with startups and established businesses?

Yes. Startups usually need a focused first version of a product; established businesses usually need to modernise or automate existing work. The process adapts to both.

Can one project combine several services?

Often. A SaaS product might combine full-stack development with an AI feature and some automation. We scope it as one coherent system.
